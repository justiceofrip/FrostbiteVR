# Cycle selection and diagnostic boundary

The installed player build remains 0.1.103 while this source batch is validated.
Its LMG supply/disposal, chest knife and empty-hand display fixes are retained.

The shared bolt driver can now stop after a real shot and preserve an unfinished
stroke in its result. Its optional switch continuation requires typed blocker and
native-origin evidence; it cannot treat equal ammunition counts as continuity.
The four affected Python suites pass 108 tests with one optional saved-run skip.

The fixed eight-slot native cycle bank retains each service's identity, ammunition
and guard history. An observed and drained departure can park a bolt obligation
while another pump or bolt weapon operates; exact return requires fresh hands and
a full new gesture. Slot generations bind queued work to its original service.
Only fully settled services may be reclaimed; unfinished debts are never evicted.
Deterministic cases exercise independent B operation and exact A return;
actual-game acceptance remains open.
Parking an unfinished pump action is not enabled by this change.

Production ordinary selection refuses departure while its current stroke is owed,
before parking or removing authority, so the player can still finish that stroke.
An issued selector can be ignored by BC2; no verified cancellation receipt exists.
The proposed predeparture activation and bookkeeping-based cancellation were
therefore removed. An observed identical selection or a timeout is not cancellation.
Complete the stroke before the ordinary swap in the next actual bolt-family check.

The optional queue/input observer is read-only, bounded and exact-executable-pinned.
An unrecorded interval crossing activation explicitly prevents a complete-window
claim. Recorded ordering does not authorize queue mutation or establish lifetime.
