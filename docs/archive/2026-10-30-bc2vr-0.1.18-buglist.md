# BC2 VR 0.1.18 headset feedback

Reported October 30, 2026 against installed private 0.1.18. The user tested
multiple guns, including an unidentified pump shotgun, probably a PKM, and an
AN-94. Exact configurations are not identified. These observations apply to the
tested weapons and actions; the PKM identification is tentative.

## Confirmed progress

- Two-hand grips work on multiple weapons, including the shotgun and LMG.
- Holster-based weapon swaps feel reliable; the LMG can swap with the weapon
  described as "m8".

## Supported source findings

- The counter reads `ReadPublishedReserve`; the shared reserve path still uses
  magazine-profile matching or `IsDiagnosticSpasConfig`. Feed-family ammunition
  has its own native view but is not connected to that counter publication.
- `ReadBodyAmmoRenderSource` has magazine, shell and fixed-container paths, but
  no feed-family chest-ammo source. This is a real implementation gap; it does
  not identify the separate native gate that prevented a particular reload.
- Empty-hand stowing requires visibility admission beyond two-hand support and
  ordinary weapon swapping. PKM has no corresponding visibility descriptor.
- Normal translated-muzzle coverage remains SPAS12/XM8 plus generated Nambu.
  The expanded support registry does not grant LMG muzzle correction. This gap
  needs addressing, but the saved logs do not establish projectile origin or aim.
- AN-94 magazine profiles are compiled. Its reload failure is unresolved rather
  than proof that it has no profile. Its underbarrel has no weapon-mode or sight
  registration, unlike the supported XM8 configurations.
- XM8 sight interaction is implemented for `XM8_sp_s` and `XM8_sp`, with exact
  mode, rig, contact and ownership requirements. The user's failed flip is open;
  this session cannot show which condition failed or establish its exact variant.

The previous source-roster completion did not establish complete live-game
reload, ammo presentation, muzzle, stow or underbarrel integration. Keep the
two-hand and swap gains while correcting those remaining shared connections.
