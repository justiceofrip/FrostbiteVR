# BC2 VR 0.1.17 headset test bug list

Reported October 29, 2026 after testing the installed private 0.1.17 candidate. These are the tester's observed symptoms; all seven issues remain open. **Mission 1 C4 throwing blocks progression.**

## Open bugs

| ID | Issue | Reported behavior and impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 017-01 | Destruction rendering | The building destruction render bug still exists. The exact building and visual artifact were not specified in this report. |
| 017-02 | Ammo counter flashing in cutscenes | The ammo counter flashes during a cutscene. |
| 017-03 | Scope view during the second level cutscene | The cutscene on the second level appears to look through a scope. |
| 017-04 | Cutscene camera positioning | Cutscene cameras are still "all over the place." Framing and orientation remain unreliable. |
| 017-05 | Mission 1 C4 throwing | **Progression blocker.** The plunger part works, but C4 cannot be thrown, preventing progress through the mission. Working plunger interaction does not resolve the throwing failure. |
| 017-06 | Choppy automatic fire | Guns now feel choppy when firing. The automatic weapon described as "m8" feels almost bursty. This is a firing-feel report; actual shot cadence and the exact weapon variant remain to be checked. |
| 017-07 | Severe performance problems | Performance in the headset is described as "horrid," despite the image looking sharp. No measured frame rate was supplied. |

## Improvements and startup observations

- Weapon holstering and interactions feel much more reliable.
- The image looks sharp in the headset.
- BC2 is no longer black with the headset connected. This supersedes the black-screen blocker for the reported connected-headset test; it does not establish the cause of the earlier black output.

## Possible connections to investigate

The tester wonders whether the sharp image comes at a performance cost, and whether poor performance causes the bursty firing sensation. Both connections are unconfirmed. Keep the performance and firing reports separate until timing evidence can distinguish frame pacing from actual shot cadence.

## Follow-up order

1. Restore Mission 1 C4 throwing and verify that the player can progress.
2. Correlate this test's logs with the performance and automatic-fire symptoms before changing quality or weapon behavior.
3. Reproduce the cutscene ammo flashing, second-level scope view and camera problems as distinct issues.
4. Identify the affected destruction scene and compare it with the existing rendering evidence.

Preserve the reported holstering and interaction gains while addressing these bugs. Logs are stored locally under `G:\Unity\VRMMO\BC2VR-Launcher\data\runtime`; this report does not yet associate the symptoms with a specific log episode.
