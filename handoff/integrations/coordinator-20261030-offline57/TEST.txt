Useful headset checks:

  magazine, release it, take a replacement from the ammo pouch with a fresh grip,
  then align and seat it. Check ammunition and post-reload firing.
- Belt-fed LMG, partly loaded and empty: open cover, remove container, take replacement
  ammunition from the chest pouch, attach container, seat belt, close cover.
  Release/regrip between steps. Hold container/belt briefly at its destination before
  releasing. This sequence has no separate latch or charging-handle step.
- Tube/pump shotgun: complete any owed rearward-and-forward pump stroke before loading;
  take and insert one shell at a time, releasing/regripping for each shell.
- Test a magazine-fed pistol separately; a slide action is only needed when its
  supported after-feed policy requires it.
- Hold XM8 automatic fire and assess both shooting cadence and general frame smoothness.
- Check LMG bullet alignment and destruction appearance; their previous fixes are retained.
