Mission 1: selecting C4 with a rifle stowed

BC2 can select its opening Expack while the rifle is still stowed. The holster previously kept the old rifle's fire suppression active because it accepted only long guns as the replacement. The repair recognizes the actual carried Expack, keeps the rifle in its slot, and retires its suppression only after neutral input and a fresh two-eye observation of the new equipment. It does not draw either item for the player. A mismatched owner, slot, inventory, equipment identity, missing view or held trigger cannot free the weapon.

This state suppressed both the place-charge action and the plunger. The original headset report described a working plunger and failed placement, so this repair alone does not establish that the reported mission stop is gone. BC2 maps the opening charge action to Zoom (bit 14); the mod routes the right B button there from its ordinary reload action. The right trigger remains the plunger. The standard reload bit (29) and the Fire axis (8) are separate. No explosives, ammunition or mission progress are granted by the mod.

The shared holster suite covers the exact script-selected charge, keeps the old slot and verifies the two cache controls separately. Both authored charge models carry the expected visible child, also present in all 96 retained selected-Expack rig samples. Those samples contain no actual switch out of a stowed rifle. Mission placement and detonation still require in-game evidence.

During normal play the local gameplay report now retains up to 32 C4 control observations: B or trigger transitions, first-seen held buttons and at most one later qualifying commit from the same continuous B hold. No separate logging switch is required. Each observation distinguishes the controller input, the game's selected item, any holster block and the final input-cache values after suppression. Unavailable reads remain unknown; the game thread can drop a diagnostic row instead of waiting on the report writer. Even a confirmed cache write is not an acknowledgement that a charge was placed. No per-frame readback or raw screenshot is collected.
