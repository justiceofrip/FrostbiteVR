Shared ordinary-XR reload stimulus pipeline

Entry point: tools/drive_bc2_reload_stimulus.py
Geometry and boundaries: tools/bc2_reload_stimulus_geometry.py
Portable checks: tests/test_bc2_reload_stimulus.py and
                 tests/test_bc2_reload_stimulus_geometry.py

This developer tool sends bounded ordinary left-controller gestures into the
installed mod. Its native input, claim, mechanism and ammunition checks remain
unchanged. The older diagnostic drivers and their non-authorizing plans remain
separate. This runner creates its own explicit ordinary-input contract from the
existing measured feed, pump, fixed-container and sight geometry adapters.

Ordinary weapon selection uses tools/bc2_draw_admission.py and the injectable
tools/bc2_draw_readiness_driver.py adapter. A brief unevaluated Held sample may
permit bounded observation with zero input; it cannot authorize a command.
Continuing requires fresh complete mapping and the same owner/item/hand claim.
The caller supplies the existing full admission/transport checks and carries
one readiness budget from Prime through draw. Portable policy/adapter tests
also support optional replay of retained actual-game evidence.

The flow is: review the actual world -> fresh selected mechanism mapping ->
neutral contact/acquisition -> bounded gesture and dwell -> owned release ->
fresh mechanism/custody/result observation. Geometry-changing steps require a
new mapping. Supply pickup and insertion share one invocation so custody is
observed continuously. Missing snapshots during motion are recorded as incomplete
evidence; they do not authorize another stage or establish a native reload.

Only left pose/grip and initial left follow-off/zero-aim-trim setup are allowed.
Head, right hand and other controls stay fixed. A competing owner, changed
selection/reference, lost focus/tracking, bad acknowledgement or deadline stops
the experiment. Cleanup only releases an acknowledged grip owned by this runner.

Example, after a root-owned actual-game session and its fresh world review:
  python -B tools/drive_bc2_reload_stimulus.py --run <session-directory>
    --profiles <session-directory>/profiles.json --family feed --stage OpenCover
    --root-authorized-ordinary-input

For feed AttachContainer, pump ShellInsert or fixed Insert, add --pickup-first.
This performs the matching pickup and fresh custody boundary before insertion.
Feed reload order is OpenCover, DetachContainer, paired pickup/AttachContainer,
SeatFeed, CloseCover. Profiles select the weapon geometry; no weapon-name branch
is needed. SightFlip uses --family sight and --target primary or secondary.

Exit0 means the requested stimulus and required fresh boundaries completed.
Inspect observationIncomplete separately. Native acceptance still requires the
actual operation/request/receipt, owner/lifetime, custody and ammunition join;
headset acceptance requires a player test. No firing is available in this tool.
